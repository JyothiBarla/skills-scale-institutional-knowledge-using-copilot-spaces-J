# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads design and execute quality strategies, ensuring features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They collaborate with product and engineering teams to validate solutions and identify risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and define testing strategies for features and releases
- Create and maintain test cases and acceptance criteria validation
- Execute manual and coordinate automated testing
- Identify quality risks and escalate blockers
- Coordinate with developers on reproducible issues and bug tracking
- Participate in sprint planning to estimate testing effort

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal production defects
- Shift-left: identify quality issues early in development
- Maintain test coverage and automation for critical paths

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Daily standups with delivery team
- Quality reports and defect logs
- Pre-release verification checklists

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads design systems and solutions, mentor developers, and ensure technical decisions align with long-term strategy and best practices. They own technical risks and propose mitigations.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and architecture for features
- Review designs and code for quality, performance, and maintainability
- Mentor junior developers and conduct design/code reviews
- Identify and escalate technical risks and dependencies
- Collaborate with Product on trade-offs between features and technical debt
- Estimate technical complexity and resource needs

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant solutions
- Build team capability through mentorship
- Balance feature velocity with technical health

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and technical mentoring sessions
- Risk register updates for technical risks
- Sprint planning for technical estimates

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Release Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and release processes. They ensure reliable, secure, and traceable deployments to production environments.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure, configuration, and environment parity
- Coordinate release planning and deployment schedules
- Execute or automate deployments and post-deployment verification
- Lead incident response and rollback procedures
- Document and maintain runbooks for deployments and incidents
- Implement security scanning and compliance checks in CI/CD

### Goals
- Minimize deployment risk and human error
- Reduce time-to-production for features
- Maintain high availability and reliability
- Support fast incident recovery and rollback capability

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and pre-deployment coordination
- Incident response and post-mortems
- Infrastructure and deployment documentation
- CI/CD metrics and observability dashboards

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business stakeholders and the delivery team. They clarify requirements, document workflows, and ensure solutions address business needs and success metrics.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Create user stories, workflows, and process documentation
- Clarify acceptance criteria and success metrics with Product and engineering
- Validate solutions align with business goals
- Identify process improvements and optimization opportunities
- Support stakeholder communication and change management

### Goals
- Ensure solutions deliver measurable business value
- Reduce miscommunication between business and technical teams
- Document processes for future reference and compliance
- Support data-driven decision making

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirements workshops
- Backlog refinement and acceptance criteria definition
- Process documentation and workflow diagrams
- Metrics and impact reporting

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, prioritization, and approval authority. They represent customer needs, business strategy, and organizational constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Provide prioritization guidance and trade-off decisions
- Approve scope, timeline, and resource commitments
- Remove organizational blockers and barriers
- Review progress and provide feedback
- Participate in decision gates (go/no-go)

### Goals
- Ensure project aligns with business strategy
- Maximize ROI and business impact
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and expectations
- Support team success through sponsorship

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and decision gates
- Monthly stakeholder updates and reviews
- Escalation and decision-making forums
- Post-release impact and metrics reporting
