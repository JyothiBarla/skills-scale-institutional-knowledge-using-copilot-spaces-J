## Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow a structured six-phase lifecycle designed to ensure customer value delivery, stakeholder alignment, and continuous improvement. Here's how projects move through each phase:

### 1. **[Initiation](octoacme-project-initiation.md)** 
Validate the business need and get stakeholder alignment.
- Confirm business need and measurable outcomes
- Identify stakeholders and champions
- Create a lightweight Project One-pager
- Make a go/no-go decision to move to planning

### 2. **[Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md)** 
Break work into shippable increments and create your roadmap.
- Conduct project kickoff with stakeholders and team
- Build a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria
- Estimate scope and define Definition of Done
- Identify dependencies and create a release plan

### 3. **[Execution & Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)** 
Manage day-to-day delivery and monitor progress toward milestones.
- Execute daily standups and weekly delivery syncs
- Use GitHub Projects or similar board for workflow management
- Follow pull request and code review standards
- Track velocity, quality metrics, and key success indicators

### 4. **[Risk & Communication](octoacme-risks-and-communication.md)** 
Identify risks, manage dependencies, and keep stakeholders informed.
- Maintain a risk register throughout the project
- Escalate blockers and dependencies promptly
- Provide regular stakeholder updates and status reports
- Follow clear communication and escalation paths

### 5. **[Release & Deployment](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md)** 
Release features to production safely and consistently.
- Prepare pre-release requirements and smoke tests
- Execute deployment with rollback plans in place
- Verify production health and announce to stakeholders
- Document release notes and known issues

### 6. **[Retrospective & Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)** 
Capture learnings and iterate on processes.
- Conduct post-project or post-sprint retrospectives
- Identify what went well and what can improve
- Create action items with clear owners and due dates
- Track and measure the impact of improvements

## Core Principles

OctoAcme's project management approach is grounded in these principles:

- **Customer-first** — Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative delivery** — Deliver small, testable increments to reduce risk and enable feedback
- **Clear ownership** — Each project has named Project Manager and Product Lead with clear accountability
- **Data-informed** — Measure impact and iterate based on evidence and metrics
- **Psychological safety** — Foster a culture of feedback, learning, and continuous improvement

## Key Artifacts

Throughout a project, you'll create and maintain these artifacts:

- **Project Charter / One-pager** — Business case and high-level plan
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** — Milestones and delivery schedule
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** — Prioritized work with acceptance criteria
- **Risk Register** — Identified risks, impact, and mitigation plans
- **Status Reports** — Weekly updates for stakeholders
- **Retrospective Notes** — Learnings and action items for continuous improvement

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily:** Team standups (15 min)
- **Weekly:** PM + PdM alignment sync and delivery team standup
- **Weekly:** Risk register review and blocker escalation
- **Bi-weekly/Monthly:** Stakeholder updates and demos
- **Ad-hoc:** Incident communication and critical escalations
